The LED

The XIAO ESP32-C6 user LED sits on GPIO15 and is wired active low: writing LOW lights it. That catches people out, so the sketch names the states instead of using the levels directly:

#define LED_PIN 15
#define LED_ON  LOW
#define LED_OFF HIGH

If your board lights up when it should be dark, swap the last two lines. If it does nothing at all, your board uses a different pin — check its pinout and change LED_PIN.

The blink is deliberately asymmetric, 200 ms on and 800 ms off. A short flash in a long gap is unmistakable, so you can tell "my sketch is running" from "the board is sitting in the bootloader" at a glance across a desk.

When the upload fails

Three failures account for nearly all of them.

SymptomCause and fix
esp_ieee802154.h: No such file later on, or the C6 not listed in the board menu Arduino-ESP32 core 2.x. The C6 needs core 3.0.0 or later. Boards Manager → esp32 → update.
"No serial data received", or the port disappears mid-upload Force bootloader mode: hold BOOT, tap RESET, release BOOT. The port comes back under a different number — reselect it and upload again.
No port appears at all Charge-only USB cable, or a hub that does not pass data. Try a different cable directly into the machine.
Why bother

Chapter 3 switches on a radio that may legitimately have nothing to hear. "No output" will be ambiguous from then on, and it is much cheaper to rule out the board now than to debug a sniffer and a toolchain at the same time.
